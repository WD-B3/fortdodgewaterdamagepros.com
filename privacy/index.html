Information we receive

This site has no forms, so it does not collect personal details through the pages. When you call the phone number, we receive your phone number and whatever you tell us about the loss, such as your address and the type of damage. If you email us, we receive your email address and your message.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How information is used

We use what you tell us to understand the loss, route the call to a restoration professional and follow up on the job. We do not use call details for anything unrelated to your service request.

Website data

Like most sites, the hosting provider may log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. That data helps keep the site running and secure.
