What to have ready before you call from Gowrie

A short, clear description saves minutes. Know the street address, the room with water and whether it is still running. Say whether the water came from a pipe, an appliance, the roof or the ground, because that decides the plan and the safety steps.

Mention anything unusual, such as a sagging ceiling, a buzzing outlet or a smell of gas. If you can, take two or three photos from the doorway before anyone starts moving things. They help your insurer later and help the crew plan the first hour.

If you rent in Gowrie, tell your landlord at the same time. The landlord usually decides who is called for the building, and your own renters policy may cover your belongings.

After the water is out

Extraction is only the first step. Wet drywall, flooring and framing keep holding moisture, and the house can look fine while it is still wet inside. Drying equipment runs for several days, and we read the walls and floors with meters until they match dry reference readings.

Gowrie homes of any age can hide wet insulation. Ask the crew to show you the readings each visit, and ask what the dry goal is for each material before equipment is removed.
