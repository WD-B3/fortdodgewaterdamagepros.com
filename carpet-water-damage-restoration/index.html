Whether the carpet stays depends on the water category, how long it sat and the carpet itself. The line is open 24/7, and same-day response is offered when available.

This page covers carpet and pad. Hard floors are handled on the hardwood floor page, and standing water is covered under extraction.

Carpet and pad

Water goes through carpet quickly and soaks into the pad beneath. The pad holds water against the subfloor, so a carpet can feel damp on top and be soaked underneath. In most clean-water losses, the pad is the first thing to go while the carpet can sometimes be saved.

The carpet backing matters too. Many modern carpets have a synthetic backing that tolerates water better than older jute backing. We check both the carpet and the pad before deciding.

How the drying works

We start with extraction using powerful units that pull water from the carpet and pad. If the pad is to be removed, the carpet is lifted, the pad is cut out and discarded, and the carpet is propped to dry with air movers. The subfloor is dried too, and moisture is checked with meters.

In some cases, carpet can be dried in place using special mats and techniques. That is a decision for the crew after seeing the loss. Afterward, the carpet may need cleaning and a new pad installed.

Why carpet that looks dry still causes trouble

Wet carpet and pad can hold moisture even if the surface feels dry. That leads to odors, staining and, if it is left alone, microbial growth. The point of measured drying is to make sure the materials reach their drying goals, so they stay dry after the equipment leaves.

If a carpet smells musty after a leak, the pad is usually the cause. Early attention costs less than replacing a carpet, a pad and a subfloor patch later.

Fort Dodge specifics

Many homes have carpet over wood or concrete subfloors, and basements are a common place for carpet near a floor drain. If your carpet sits on a basement floor, the concrete beneath it can stay damp for a long time. The basement page covers what to expect.

Humid Fort Dodge summers slow drying, and cold winters make it harder to heat a space for drying. We adjust equipment and temperature to the season. Carpet in a nearby town such as Eagle Grove gets the same treatment.

What affects the cost of carpet restoration

Carpet costs are driven by:

  • Water category: Contaminated water means replacement
  • Time wet: Longer soaks mean more replacement
  • Area: The square footage of wet carpet
  • Pad replacement: Pad is usually replaced
  • Subfloor drying: Wet subfloor needs more drying time

A sudden leak that soaks carpet is often covered by a homeowners policy, while slow leaks and flood often are not. Check your policy or ask your insurer. Want your real number? Call the line.

What to do with the carpet while you wait

Keep foot traffic off wet carpet, since walking on it pushes water into the pad and spreads dirt. Lift furniture legs onto foil or plastic blocks so wood stain and rust do not transfer to the fibers. Remove loose rugs and small items to a dry room.

Do not run a household vacuum on wet carpet. Turn off the heating vents in the room if they are blowing dust and debris onto the wet surface, and open interior doors so air can move. If the water was gray or black, keep children and pets out of the room entirely.

Take photos of the carpet, the pad and the water line on the walls before anything is lifted. Those pictures, together with the moisture log from the crew, support the insurance file and show what was dried and what was removed.

Safety steps while you wait

Whether carpet is dried or replaced is decided under the ANSI/IICRC S500 guidance. Until the crew arrives, these steps keep people safe.

  • Stay off wet carpet to keep from pushing water into the pad.
  • Keep children and pets out if the water was gray or black.
  • Do not use a household vacuum on wet carpet.

Questions about carpet water damage in Fort Dodge

Can wet carpet be saved?

Often, if the water was clean and the carpet is extracted and dried quickly. The pad is usually replaced.

How long can carpet stay wet before it must go?

Clean-water carpet can often be saved within about a day or two. After that, replacement becomes more likely.

Will the carpet smell?

A musty smell usually means the pad or subfloor is still damp. Proper drying removes it.

Do I need to replace carpet after a flood?

Yes. Flood and sewage water contaminate carpet, and it is generally replaced.
