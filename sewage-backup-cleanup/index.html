Sewage backup cleanup in Fort Dodge, IA deals with Category 3 water, the most hazardous kind. It can carry bacteria and other contaminants, so the work is done with protective equipment and strict containment. The emergency line is open 24/7, and same-day response is offered when available.

This page is only for sewage and drain backups. Floodwater from a river or creek is handled differently and is covered under flood damage cleanup. A clean supply line break is on the burst pipe page.

Sewage backup: what to do first

Stay out of the affected area. Sewage is a health hazard, and entering the room spreads contamination on shoes and clothing. If water is near electrical outlets or the panel, call the utility or 911 before you call us.

Stop using water

Do not run sinks, showers, toilets or washing machines. Every flush sends more water into the blocked line.

Keep people and pets out

Children, elderly people and anyone with a weakened immune system should leave the area. Pets should stay out too.

Do not clean it yourself

Do not use household vacuums or a wet vac. They spread contamination and can be damaged by the water.

Call the line and the city if needed

Call us for cleanup. If the blockage might be in the city main, ask the city about it.

Sewage calls at night and on weekends are routine. Same-day response is offered when available.

What does a sewage backup look like?

Many people do not search for Category 3 water by name. They notice gurgling drains, a toilet that rises when the washer drains, dark water coming up in a basement floor drain or a strong smell near a shower. Those are the symptoms of a sewage backup, and the trade term for the job is sewage backup cleanup under Category 3 water protocols.

If a floor drain is bubbling or water is rising in the lowest fixture in the house, treat it as a backup.

How the cleanup works

  1. Contain. Crews set barriers or plastic sheeting to keep contamination from spreading.
  2. Extract. Pumps and extraction units remove liquid, and solids are cleared.
  3. Remove. Porous materials such as carpet, pad and soaked drywall are cut out and bagged.
  4. Clean and treat. Hard surfaces are cleaned and treated with an antimicrobial product.
  5. Dry. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure, and readings are logged.

Industry practice for contaminated water follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, and you can ask any company whether its crews work to it. HEPA air filtration is sometimes used to reduce airborne particles during removal.

Why backups happen

Common causes include a clog in your own lateral line, tree roots, a collapsed pipe, a blockage in the city main and heavy rain overwhelming the system. A plumber can run a camera down the line to find the cause. Whether it is your line or the city's decides who fixes it.

Ask your insurer about water and sewer backup coverage. The Iowa Insurance Division notes that backup is separate from flood insurance and usually needs an endorsement on a homeowners policy.

Fort Dodge specifics

In Fort Dodge, the city runs the public water and sewer system, and its municipal code has a title devoted to water and sewers. If you suspect the blockage is in the city main, contact the city. If it is in your lateral, a plumber or a drain contractor handles it.

In Humboldt, the city's water and wastewater department contracts out day-to-day operations, and in Eagle Grove the city has a page on sewer backups. If you live outside Fort Dodge, call your own city first about the main.

What affects the cost of sewage cleanup

Because of the safety steps, sewage jobs usually cost more than clean-water jobs. The factors are:

  • Area contaminated: The square footage sets the removal and cleaning scope
  • Materials removed: Carpet, pad and drywall come out
  • Protective measures: Containment, protective gear and disposal add time
  • Drying days: The structure still needs to dry after cleaning
  • Rebuild scope: Replacing removed materials follows

Sewer and water backup is often excluded from a standard homeowners policy unless you added an endorsement. Check your policy or ask your insurer. Want your real number? Call the line.

Is sewage water dangerous?

How do I know if the backup is from the city or my own line?

A plumber can run a camera to find it. If the blockage is in the city main, the city responds. If it is in your lateral, it is your responsibility.

What do you call the symptom in plain words?
